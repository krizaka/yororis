# Security Policy

Yororis is **under construction**: nothing is deployed, there is no production service and no account outside the
maintainers. A report still matters — the code here is what will one day hold the most private data of the
organisation.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report privately through [GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/krizaka/yororis/security/advisories/new) or by
e-mail to **security@krizaka.com**. Do not open a public issue. We acknowledge reports within 72 hours.

## What the design already commits to

- Every AI call goes through Orazaka's wellbeing pack; no other provider.
- A moment's transcript is never kept beyond its summary; summaries and the thread are encrypted at rest.
- Mood is self-reported, never inferred.
- Production refuses to start without `APP_RELEASE_APPROVAL`, the reference of a written and signed decision.

## Not an emergency service

Yororis is not an emergency service. If you or someone near you is in danger, call your local emergency number. In
Canada, call or text **9-8-8**; in Québec, **811** (Info-Social); in France, **3114**; in Belgium, **0800 32 123**
(French) or **1813** (Dutch).
